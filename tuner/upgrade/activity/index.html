§Deep Active-Work & OS Process Tree Inspector

§Overview

Detects in-flight foreground turns, background SessionAsyncObserver runs, scheduled cron and quiet heartbeat executions, OS /proc descendant tool processes (including direct one-shot CLI runs), and delegates Antigravity recursive subagent/task inspection.
