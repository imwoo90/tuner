§Antigravity Subagent & Background Task Brain Inspector

§Overview

Walks live Antigravity conversation IDs and their recursive SUBAGENT_STATE_ALIVE child subagent descriptors on disk, streaming compact JSONL steps in monotonic step_index order to detect mid-turn execution, live timers, and sentinel crons while excluding suppressed HeartbeatScheduler self-check turns from idle epochs.
