§Antigravity Subagent, Native Swarm & Background Task Brain Inspector

§Overview

Walks live Antigravity conversation IDs, recursive SUBAGENT_STATE_ALIVE child subagent descriptors, and agy 1.5.7+ Native Swarm swarm/members.json rosters on disk, streaming compact JSONL steps in monotonic step_index order to detect mid-turn execution, unstarted working swarm members, parked swarm_wait turns, live timers, and sentinel crons while excluding quiet HeartbeatScheduler turns.
