§Real-Time Voice Desk Assistant WebSocket Bridge (voice_ws.rs)

§Overview

Authenticates /dashboard/api/voice/ws and bridges browser 16 kHz PCM microphone audio and 24 kHz PCM speech output with Gemini Live (BidiGenerateContent), executing front-desk tools server-side without ever exposing GEMINI_API_KEY to browser JavaScript.
