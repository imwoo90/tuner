§Web Turn Execution & Telegram Mirroring Dispatch (turn_runner.rs)

§Overview

Acquires per-topic LockPool exclusivity for Web-initiated turns, resolves inline @model directives, drains pending uploads, mirrors [🌐 Web] messages into Telegram forum topics when mirrored, and delegates stream consumption to super::turn_stream.
