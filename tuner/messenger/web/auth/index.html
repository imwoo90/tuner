§Web Dashboard Authentication, PIN Hashing & Brute-Force Guard (auth.rs)

§Overview

Manages authentication operations over persistent 0600 state (dashboard_auth.json): single-use 256-bit Magic Links (10m TTL), PBKDF2_HMAC_SHA256 (100,000 rounds) PINs, 30-day sliding cookies with CSRF tokens, and per-IP 429 lockouts.
