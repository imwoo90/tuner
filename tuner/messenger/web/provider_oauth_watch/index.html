§Web-in-Web CLI OAuth Auto-Harvester & Completion Watcher (provider_oauth_watch.rs)

§Overview

Polls active Web-in-Web HITL Chrome sessions (cli_antigravity, cli_claude, cli_codex) over loopback IPC to automatically harvest PKCE authorization codes ([data-code-box] or ?code=) into the waiting CLI PTY, auto-saving & closing the remote browser once authenticated.
