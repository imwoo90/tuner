§Antigravity Turn Slice Scanner & Async Input Contention Guard (turn_settle.rs)

§Overview

Prevents foreground user input from colliding with background/post-turn SYSTEM_MESSAGE steps on warm agy PTYs by settling active background turns prior to stdin injection, anchoring turn completion to the trailing JSONL state, and preserving both background and user answers.
