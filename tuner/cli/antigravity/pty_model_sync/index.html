§Antigravity Warm PTY Model & Default-Reset Synchronizer (pty_model_sync.rs)

§Overview

Synchronizes /model <target> directives onto ready interactive agy PTYs and guards antigravity-default PTY resets against active or parked subagent trees, Native Swarms, live timers/tasks, and child OS processes.
