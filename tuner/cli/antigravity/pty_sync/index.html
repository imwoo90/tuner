§Antigravity PTY Initialization & Model Sync Coordinator (pty_sync.rs)

§Overview

Serializes post-OAuth interactive PTY prompt readiness (wait_for_pty_prompt) per session ID, tracks the /model <target> applied to each live PTY OS PID, and guards in-flight topic turns and open ask_question TUI selectors against concurrent process termination or raw stdin /model injection.
